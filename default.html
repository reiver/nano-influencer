Nano-Influencer

an influencer with 1,000 to 10,000 followers who has who has a high level of trust and engagement within a specific niche

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

A nano-influencer is an influencer with 1,000 to 10,000 followers who has who has a high level of trust and engagement within a specific niche.

Nano-Influencers (and micro-influencers) are attractive to marketers because they can have much higher engagement rates than macro-influencers — and a better ROI.

Influencer

A nano-influencer is a type of influencer.

A influencer is a person who has the power to shape the opinions, behaviors, or buying choices of others.

Types of Influencers

Influencers are sometimes classified in terms of their follower count or community size.

nano-influencer
an influencer with a follower count or community size of at least 1,000 but under 10,000.
micro-influencer
an influencer with a follower count or community size of at least 10,000 but under 100,000.
macro-influencer
an influencer with a follower count or community size of at least 100,000.

Influencer Marketing

Influencer Marketing is where influencers (which could be nano-influencers, micro-influencers, or macro-influencers) (directly or indirectly) promote a product, service, or view point.

Typically marketers from a brand compensates the influencer somehow to do this. Perhaps giving them money. Or, giving them equity in a ventures. Etc.

Prestige Bias

One human tendency that makes nano-influencers possible is: the prestige bias.

The prestige bias is the human tendency (mental habit) to copy, learn from, or trust people who have high social status, admiration, or respect.

The prestige bias is a shortcut habit where people assume popular or respected figures know best.

The prestige bias helps people learn useful skills quickly without testing every single person.

Success and Payoff Bias

Another human tendency that makes nano-influencers possible is: the success and payoff bias.

The success and payoff bias is a human tendency (mental habit) where individuals copy behaviors, beliefs, or strategies from people who have achieved high status, wealth, or successful outcomes. I.e., rather than a person evaluating a behavior on its own merits, they simply look at who is "winning" and imitate them.

Similarity Bias

Yet another human tendency that makes nano-influencers possible is: the similarity bias. The similarity bias is also known as the affinity bias, and the similar-to-me effect.

The similarity bias is a human tendency (mental habit) that makes people favor others who share similar traits, backgrounds, or interests. It leads to trusting or liking people who look, speak, or think like us faster than those who do not.
