Nano-Influencer

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

A nano-influencer is an influencer with 1,000 to 10,000 followers who has who has a high level of trust and engagement within a specific niche

Nano-Influencers are attractive to marketers because they can have much higher engagement rates than macro-influencers — and a better ROI.

Influencer

A nano-influencer is a type of influencer.

A influencer is a person who has the power to shape the opinions, behaviors, or buying choices of others.

Types of Influencers

Influencers are sometimes classified in terms of their follower count or community size.

nano-influencer
an influencer with a follower count or community size of at least 1,000 but under 10,000.
micro-influencer
an influencer with a follower count or community size of at least 100,000 but under 1,000,000.
macro-influencer
an influencer with a follower count or community size of at least 1,000,000.
